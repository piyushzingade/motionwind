# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Motionwind a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing
private information, or sustained disruption.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
Report incidents privately through a GitHub security advisory or to the project
owner listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
kept confidential to the extent possible.

This policy follows Contributor Covenant 2.1.
